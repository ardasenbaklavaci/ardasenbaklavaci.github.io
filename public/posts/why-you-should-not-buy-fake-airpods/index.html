Why You Should NOT Buy Fake AirPods in 2026: The Truth About Replica Earbuds

Fake AirPods have become increasingly sophisticated over the last few years. What once looked like an obvious cheap imitation has evolved into something much more convincing. Some replicas now feature surprisingly good sound quality, AirPods-style pairing animations, battery indicators, and even compatibility with certain Apple device features.

At first glance, buying a pair of replica AirPods for a fraction of the original price might seem like a great deal. After all, why spend a significant amount of money on genuine Apple AirPods when a much cheaper alternative appears to offer a similar experience?

I used to think about this differently as well.



I have personally owned replica AirPods, a pair of Airpods 3 with charging case, branded as “Fubar Airpods 3” bought from Turkey. Surprisingly, the experience was not entirely bad. The sound quality was quite good, and the earbuds were recognized by Apple devices almost like genuine AirPods. Also find my Apple was working when you connected the Fake Airpods 3 to an Apple Device…



However, after looking beyond the initial experience, I believe there are several important reasons why buying counterfeit AirPods is not a good decision.

This article is not about promoting replica products or recommending specific counterfeit models. Instead, I want to explain how these products work, why some of them are surprisingly convincing, what their technical limitations are, and why I would recommend choosing legitimate budget TWS earbuds instead.

1. What Are These Fake AirPods and Why They Became So Popular?

Fake AirPods are wireless earbuds designed to imitate Apple’s original AirPods. They may copy the appearance of the earbuds, charging case, packaging, product names, and even some of the software features.

You may see descriptions at online shopping stores like:

  • 1:1 AirPods replica

  • OEM AirPods

  • High-copy AirPods

  • Clone AirPods

  • Replica AirPods Pro

  • AirPods 3 replicas

These terms are often used loosely by sellers and do not necessarily describe a specific hardware platform or quality level.

It is also important to distinguish between generic TWS earbuds and counterfeit AirPods.

TWS stands for True Wireless Stereo. It simply describes wireless earbuds that operate without a cable connecting the left and right sides. TWS doesnt have any implementation made or mimic Apple Airpods Products. Just think like a classic cheap bluetooth wirelles in ear twin headphones. 

There are many legitimate manufacturers producing affordable TWS earbuds. These products may have a similar general design to AirPods without pretending to be genuine Apple products.

Counterfeit AirPods are different. They attempt to imitate Apple’s branding and product identity, potentially misleading buyers into believing that they are purchasing something manufactured or authorized by Apple.

Their popularity is understandable. Genuine AirPods can be expensive, while replicas are marketed at considerably lower prices.

But the low price does not necessarily mean good value.

2. Not All Fake AirPods Use the Same Hardware

One common misconception is that every replica AirPods model uses the same cheap Bluetooth chip and offers equally poor performance.

Inside of an Original Apple Airpods


That is not necessarily true.

The replica market has evolved, and different products can use different Bluetooth audio platforms. Some inexpensive models use basic solutions, while more sophisticated versions have been associated with chipset families such as Airoha, Huilian, and BES.

These platforms help explain why certain replicas can offer surprisingly convincing connectivity and audio features.

However, identifying and knowing the chipset alone is not enough to determine the quality and performance of an entire product itself.

Airoha Bluetooth Chipsets

Airoha is one of the manufacturers frequently mentioned in discussions about more advanced AirPods replicas.



Chipset names and variants reported in different replica generations include:

  • Airoha 1562M

  • Airoha 1562E

  • Airoha 1563E

  • Airoha 1571AM

Some community documentation associates these chipsets with different AirPods-style replica versions.

Airoha 1562A equipped replica Airpods Pro from Aliexpress


Depending on the implementation, Airoha-based platforms can offer relatively advanced Bluetooth connectivity, audio processing, power management, and support for additional features.

This is one reason why some replicas perform considerably better than basic generic Bluetooth earbuds.

However, a product using an Airoha chipset is not automatically high quality. The speaker drivers, microphones, firmware, battery cells, antenna design, and overall manufacturing process are equally important.

It is also worth noting that chipset names used in replica communities do not always correspond directly to official manufacturer product names. Exact hardware identification can vary between models and production batches.

Huilian Chipsets and Apple-Like Integration

Huilian is another chipset family that appears in discussions about more sophisticated AirPods replicas.

Names such as Huilian A10 and Huilian 247 have been associated with products offering advanced iOS-style integration.

Huilian 247 equipped Airpods Pro 3 from a Russian seller…


Some implementations reportedly support AirPods-like pairing experiences and features resembling Apple’s multi-device functionality.

This can make certain replicas surprisingly convincing when used with an iPhone.

However, similar-looking software behavior does not mean that a product has genuine Apple hardware or access to every proprietary Apple service.

BES (Bestechnic) Chipsets

BES is another manufacturer associated with Bluetooth audio platforms used in different wireless earbuds.

Examples reported in replica discussions include:

  • BES 2300ZP

  • BES 2500YP

  • BES 2600 series

Some implementations are associated with more advanced audio processing and noise-cancellation capabilities.

Nevertheless, chipset specifications alone cannot guarantee effective ANC, good microphone quality, or long-term reliability.

A poorly designed product can still perform badly even if it uses a relatively advanced Bluetooth chipset.

Jieli and Bluetrum: Other Bluetooth Solutions

Not every replica uses a relatively advanced platform.

Jieli (JL) and Bluetrum are also associated with affordable Bluetooth audio products and entry-level wireless earbuds.

These platforms can provide basic wireless audio functionality, although the actual performance depends on the complete hardware and firmware implementation.

It is important not to judge a product’s quality, performance etc… directly by its chipset. The quality matters by the whole implemention of product including chipset, speakers, microphone and the battery case. 

A cheaper Bluetooth platform does not automatically mean terrible sound quality, just as a more expensive platform does not guarantee a good listening experience.

The biggest issue is the lack of reliable information and quality control surrounding many counterfeit products.

5. Sound Quality: Some Replicas Are Surprisingly Good, But Consistency Matters

Sound quality is one of the most important factors when purchasing wireless earbuds.

As my Fubar AirPods 3 experience demonstrates, some replicas can sound surprisingly good.

However, this does not mean that the entire replica market offers consistent audio performance.

Depending on the product, you may encounter:

  • Weak or uncontrolled bass

  • Muddy midrange frequencies

  • Harsh or overly sharp treble

  • Distortion at higher volumes

  • Poor or zero stereo separation that gives mono sound on both pair

  • Different sound levels between earbuds which give trash experience

The biggest problem is inconsistency.

Two products advertised with similar descriptions may use completely different drivers, chipsets, and firmware according to the production batch difference.

You also cannot reliably determine sound quality from the seller's product photographs or specifications. Maybe from the comments from previous buyers, review star average...

Legitimate budget TWS manufacturers generally provide more identifiable product information and offer a better opportunity to find independent reviews.

You do not need to spend a fortune to get decent sound quality. 

You simply need a product from a manufacturer that stands behind what it sells.

6. Battery Life and Charging Reliability

Battery performance is another area where inexpensive counterfeit earbuds can become problematic.

Wireless earbuds contain small rechargeable batteries, and the charging case contains an additional battery.

Their performance depends on several factors:

  • Battery cell quality

  • Charging circuit design

  • Power management

  • Firmware optimization

  • Manufacturing consistency

Some replicas may initially offer acceptable battery life but become less reliable after repeated charging cycles.

Potential problems include:

  • One earbud draining faster than the other

  • Inaccurate battery percentage readings

  • Charging cases losing capacity quickly

  • Earbuds failing to charge consistently

  • Unexpected shutdowns

There is also a safety consideration.

Lithium-ion batteries require trusted charging protection and thermal control. Poorly manufactured or inadequately protected battery systems can overheating, swelling and create fire hazard.

This does not mean that every inexpensive wireless earbud is dangerous.

However, when buying a Replica product you need to know that it doesnt have certificates for safety. 

Saving money is not particularly useful if the product becomes unreliable or potentially unsafe.

7. Fake ANC and Misleading Product Features

Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) is one of the most attractive features of modern wireless earbuds.

It uses microphones and electronic processing to reduce certain types of environmental noise.

Unfortunately, some replica products advertise features such as:

  • Transparency Mode

  • Spatial Audio

  • Adaptive Audio

  • Environmental Noise Cancellation

It is also important to distinguish between active noise cancellation and passive noise isolation.



Passive isolation comes from the physical fit of the earbuds and their ear tips.

ANC uses microphones and signal processing to reduce external sounds. ANC is a specification which requires professional and reliable implementation. 

Some inexpensive earbuds may offer basic passive isolation but very limited active noise cancellation.

Without credible specifications and independent measurements, it is difficult to know what you are actually getting.

A convincing feature list is not the same as a properly implemented feature.

10. Build Quality, Durability and Warranty Support

The initial appearance of a replica can be surprisingly convincing.

The charging case may look similar to the original, the earbuds may feel reasonably solid, and the packaging may imitate Apple's design.

But appearance is not a reliable indicator of long-term durability.

Potential problems include:

  • Loose charging contacts

  • Weak case hinges

  • Damaged charging ports

  • Earbuds that stop charging

  • Worn-out batteries

  • Unreliable touch controls

Another important issue is after-sales support.

With a genuine product or a legitimate budget earbud from an established manufacturer, you generally have a clearer route to warranty service, replacement, and technical support.

With an unknown counterfeit product, support may be limited or completely unavailable.

If something breaks after a few months, you may have no practical way to repair or replace it.

This is one of the biggest reasons I would avoid counterfeit electronics, even when their initial performance seems acceptable.

11. Not All Fake AirPods Are Sold as Apple Products

Another category worth discussing is branded AirPods replicas.

Unlike products that directly use Apple's logo and packaging, these earbuds are sometimes sold under their own brand names or alternative product names. They may use almost identical designs, similar charging cases, and marketing language that deliberately reminds customers of Apple's AirPods.

Subzero Air 3 Wirelles Airpoids. Replica airpods selling as branded by "Subzero". Warranty may apply...


Some of these products have appeared in international markets under names such as WhitePods, ColorPods, and other AirPods-inspired branding.

Their packaging may look different from Apple's, but the product itself can still be designed to imitate the appearance and perceived experience of genuine AirPods.

This distinction is important: a product carrying another company's brand is not automatically a counterfeit Apple product.

There are legitimate wireless earbuds from established manufacturers that offer similar designs and features. However, some lesser-known brands use this approach to make generic or replica hardware appear more established and trustworthy than it really is.

The main concern is transparency.

Who actually manufactures the earbuds? Which chipset and Bluetooth platform do they use? Are the advertised battery capacity, noise cancellation, microphone quality, and water-resistance claims independently verified? Does the company provide firmware updates, replacement parts, customer support, and a meaningful warranty?

A brand name printed on a charging case does not automatically answer these questions.

Even if a particular pair sounds surprisingly good or connects to an iPhone with an AirPods-like pop-up, that does not establish that it offers the same hardware, software support, or long-term reliability as a genuine product.

For this reason, consumers should look beyond the branding and promotional claims.

A clearly identified, independently reviewed budget earbud from a reputable manufacturer is generally a more transparent choice than an imitation product whose actual manufacturer and specifications are difficult to verify.

It is also important not to confuse these products with legitimate alternatives from companies such as JBL, Sony, Samsung, or OnePlus. Similar design language does not automatically make a product counterfeit.

Can You Adjust EQ and ANC on Android?

Potentially, yes, but it depends on the earbuds.

There are two different types of audio adjustment worth understanding.

Software EQ: Android's built-in equalizer or a compatible third-party audio application can modify the sound output. This can work even when the earbuds do not support a dedicated companion application.


Samsung's Built In Software Custom EQ Settings 


Hardware-level controls: Some chipset-specific applications can communicate directly with compatible earbuds to change supported settings, including EQ profiles, ANC modes, or touch controls.

However, a generic Android EQ application cannot magically add genuine ANC to earbuds that do not have the necessary hardware.

Likewise, selecting an ANC mode in an application does not guarantee effective noise cancellation.

A Word of Caution About Third-Party Applications

Many chipset-specific companion applications are distributed outside the Google Play Store.

Before installing one, check its developer, source, permissions, and compatibility with your exact earbud version.

Be particularly careful with unofficial APK downloads and firmware updates. Installing incompatible firmware can cause connectivity problems or even make the earbuds unusable.

Avoid granting unnecessary permissions or installing unknown applications simply because a seller recommends them.

The important point is that some replicas offer considerably more configuration options than their appearance initially suggests. However, these features are highly dependent on the hardware and software combination.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are fake AirPods so cheap?

They may use less expensive components, simpler manufacturing processes, and fewer quality-control and support services. Counterfeit products may also imitate Apple's branding without providing genuine Apple hardware or services.
